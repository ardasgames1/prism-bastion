Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 6, 2026

Thank you for playing Prism Bastion, developed and operated by ArdasGames ("ArdasGames", "we", "our", or "us").

This Privacy Policy explains how Prism Bastion handles information when the game is used on supported platforms, including Huawei HarmonyOS, Huawei AppGallery, Wearables, Android, iOS, and other platforms on which the game may be made available.

Privacy by design: Prism Bastion is designed to minimize the collection and processing of personal information. The game does not require player account registration and does not intentionally collect personal information for ArdasGames' own independent purposes.

1. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

ArdasGames does not intentionally collect or maintain a player profile containing personal information through Prism Bastion.

The game does not require account registration and, through its own functionality, does not intentionally request or collect your:

  • name;
  • email address;
  • telephone number;
  • postal or physical address;
  • GPS or precise location information;
  • contacts or address book information;
  • microphone recordings;
  • camera or photo content; or
  • other information submitted specifically to identify you.

ArdasGames does not sell, rent, or trade personal information and does not operate a behavioral advertising or personal profiling system within Prism Bastion.

2. Local Game Data

Gameplay-related information, including level progress, high scores, campaign unlocks, game settings, sound settings, and other game-state information, is stored locally on the player's device.

ArdasGames does not maintain a remote player profile or cloud database containing this gameplay data.

Local game data may be deleted when the game is uninstalled, application data is cleared, the device is reset, or the operating system otherwise removes application data. ArdasGames cannot recover locally stored gameplay data that has been deleted from the device.

3. In-App Purchases and Payments

Prism Bastion may offer optional digital purchases, such as a full campaign unlock.

Payments are processed by the applicable platform provider, such as Huawei In-App Purchases (IAP), Google Play Billing, or Apple App Store services, depending on the platform and distribution channel.

ArdasGames does not request or intentionally store payment card numbers, bank account credentials, or other payment credentials. Payment providers may independently process transaction information, purchase identifiers, receipts, platform-account information, or other information required to process and verify a purchase.

Such processing is governed by the applicable platform provider's own terms and privacy policy.

4. Purchase Verification and Network Access

Where network access is required, Prism Bastion may communicate with platform services for purposes necessary to operate platform functionality, including purchase verification and related transaction services.

On Huawei HarmonyOS versions of the game, the application may request the standard network permission ohos.permission.INTERNET where required by the Huawei platform and its In-App Purchase functionality.

Network access is not used by ArdasGames for behavioral advertising, cross-application tracking, sale of personal information, or user profiling.

5. Third-Party Platform Services

Prism Bastion may rely on services provided by the platform on which the game is installed or distributed. These may include Huawei AppGallery and Huawei In-App Purchases, Google Play and Google Play Billing, Apple App Store services, and operating-system services.

These platform providers may independently process information in accordance with their own privacy policies, terms of service, and applicable laws.

ArdasGames does not control the privacy practices of these independent platform providers and is not responsible for their independent processing of information.

Nothing in this Privacy Policy is intended to limit any rights or protections provided to users under applicable law.

6. Advertising, Analytics, Tracking and Profiling

Prism Bastion does not intentionally include third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral advertising systems, cross-application tracking technologies, or user-profiling systems operated by ArdasGames.

ArdasGames does not sell personal information to advertisers, advertising networks, data brokers, or other third parties.

Platform providers may independently perform technical, security, fraud-prevention, measurement, or other processing in connection with their platform services. Such processing is governed by the relevant provider's own policies.

7. Permissions

Prism Bastion is designed to request only permissions that are necessary for the functionality of the applicable version of the game and its platform services.

The game does not intentionally request access to contacts, microphone, camera, or precise location for gameplay purposes.

Platform-specific permissions may differ between Huawei HarmonyOS, Android, iOS, Wearables, and other supported platforms.

8. Children's Privacy

Prism Bastion may be used by players of different ages. The game does not require account registration and does not intentionally collect personal information from children for ArdasGames' own purposes.

ArdasGames does not use personal information collected through Prism Bastion for behavioral advertising or targeted marketing.

ArdasGames aims to comply with applicable child privacy and data protection requirements in the jurisdictions in which Prism Bastion is offered. Nothing in this Privacy Policy constitutes a warranty that the game is exempt from every child-privacy law or regulatory requirement in every jurisdiction.

Where applicable law imposes additional requirements concerning children or minors, ArdasGames will comply with those requirements to the extent applicable to its activities.

9. Data Security

Because ArdasGames does not maintain a server-side database of player personal information through Prism Bastion, ArdasGames does not maintain a centralized repository of player personal information collected through the game.

ArdasGames nevertheless takes reasonable technical and organizational measures within its control to protect information processed through its services against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

ArdasGames does not intentionally retain a server-side personal profile for Prism Bastion players.

Gameplay data stored locally on the player's device remains subject to the storage and deletion mechanisms of the applicable operating system. Users may generally remove local game data by uninstalling the game or clearing its application data, subject to the features and behavior of the relevant platform.
