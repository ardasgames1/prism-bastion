Prism Bastion — Official Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 6, 2026

Thank you for playing Prism Bastion, developed and operated by ArdasGames ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data on Huawei HarmonyOS, Wearables, Android, iOS, and other supported platforms.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Prism Bastion does NOT collect, store, share, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not require account registration, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, location/GPS data, device contacts, or microphone/camera feeds.

2. Local Storage and Offline Gameplay

All gameplay data—including level progress, high scores, campaign unlocks, and sound settings—is stored solely on your local device storage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to external servers.

3. In-App Purchases & Payments

If you purchase the optional full campaign unlock within the game, the transaction is processed directly and securely by Huawei In-App Purchases (IAP), Google Play Billing, or Apple App Store via your platform account. We do not access, receive, or store any of your credit card details, banking credentials, or billing addresses.

4. Permissions and Internet Access

The game requests standard network permission (ohos.permission.INTERNET) strictly for the purpose of communicating with the official Huawei In-App Purchase gateway to verify your purchase receipt. We do not integrate any third-party tracking, advertising, or profiling SDKs.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K Compliant)

Prism Bastion is designed for players of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal data, the game is fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
